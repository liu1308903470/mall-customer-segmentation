# Precision Marketing Strategy Plan for Mall Customer Segmentation

## Strategy Overview
Based on the customer value segmentation results, differentiated operation strategies are formulated for 5 customer groups. The core objectives are: maintain high-value customers, activate potential groups, upgrade mass customers, and recover churn groups. The overall strategy is expected to drive the mall's monthly overall revenue growth by more than 15%.

---

## Cluster 0: VIP High-Value Group
### Customer Characteristics
- Proportion: 20%
- Labels: High income, high consumption, high frequency, mainly middle-aged
- Value contribution: Contributes approximately 45% of total revenue, serving as the core profit source

### Operation Objectives
- Improve customer loyalty and reduce churn rate
- Tap high-end consumption potential and increase average order value
- Build word-of-mouth communication and attract high-net-worth new customers

### Specific Strategies
1. **Exclusive Benefit System**
   - Launch VIP exclusive channels: free parking, exclusive checkout counters, lounge access
   - Double points + exclusive gifts during birthday month; customized services for top 100 annual spenders
2. **High-End Scenario Marketing**
   - Regular high-end tasting events: luxury goods, jewelry, wine, home design salons
   - Priority experience for new products, priority purchase right for limited editions
3. **Dedicated Services**
   - Assign 1-on-1 dedicated account managers for proactive follow-up and demand docking
   - Exclusive invitations to high-end brand private sales
4. **Reach Channels**: Dedicated account manager calls, premium member SMS, private community

### Effect Metrics
- VIP churn rate < 5%
- Annual consumption growth rate of VIP group > 8%
- High-end event participation rate > 30%

---

## Cluster 1: High-Income Low-Consumption Group (Core Growth Group)
### Customer Characteristics
- Proportion: 20%
- Labels: High income, low consumption frequency, decent AOV, huge potential
- Core issues: Inactive consumption scenarios, insufficient brand awareness, undeveloped consumption habits

### Operation Objectives
- Group activation rate ≥ 30%
- Annual consumption frequency increased by 50%
- Conversion rate to VIP group ≥ 15%

### Specific Strategies
1. **Precision Scenario Activation**
   - Push tiered benefits for corresponding categories based on browsing and consumption records
   - Issue "high-threshold high-discount" coupons matching their consumption capacity
2. **Consumption Frequency Improvement**
   - Launch monthly consumption tasks: points/parking coupons for 2 purchases per month
   - Exclusive weekday benefits to guide off-peak store visits
3. **High-End Category Penetration**
   - Push new product information and member-exclusive discounts for high-end brands
   - Invite to tasting events for peer customer groups
4. **Reach Channels**: Precision SMS, APP pop-ups, service account template messages

### Effect Metrics
- Group activation rate ≥ 30%
- Consumption frequency increase ≥ 50%
- Activity ROI ≥ 1:3.5

---

## Cluster 2: Mass Consumption Mainforce
### Customer Characteristics
- Proportion: 30%, the largest population
- Labels: Medium income, medium consumption, moderate frequency
- Value contribution: Revenue foundation, main passenger flow source

### Operation Objectives
- Stabilize passenger flow and consumption
- Increase average order value and cross-purchase rate
- Cultivate consumption habits and increase store visit frequency

### Specific Strategies
1. **Regular Promotion Operations**
   - Holiday universal tiered discounts, store anniversary events, member day discounts
   - Launch multi-category combo packages to increase attach rate
2. **Average Order Value Growth**
   - Gift with minimum spend, tiered discounts to guide basket building
   - Related recommendations: matching products at checkout page
3. **Points System Operation**
   - Points redemption for cash or gifts to strengthen points value
   - Double points on member days for periodic reactivation
4. **Reach Channels**: Official account pushes, bulk SMS, APP homepage events

### Effect Metrics
- Overall passenger flow stable
- Average order value increase ≥ 10%
- Cross-purchase rate increase ≥ 8%

---

## Cluster 3: Young New Customer Group
### Customer Characteristics
- Proportion: 16%
- Labels: Young age, low income, low consumption, high proportion of new customers
- Value: Future potential customer group, passenger flow reservoir

### Operation Objectives
- Improve new customer retention rate
- Cultivate in-store consumption habits
- Gradually upgrade consumption tier

### Specific Strategies
1. **New Customer Retention**
   - First-order gift pack: No-threshold coupon + new customer exclusive price upon registration
   - 3 store visit rewards in the first month to guide initial habit formation
2. **Lightweight Conversion**
   - Launch low-price experience products, F&B and entertainment group deals
   - Student exclusive discounts matching their consumption capacity
3. **Social Fission**
   - Rewards for inviting friends, leveraging social attributes of young groups
   - Benefits for check-in sharing to guide social media dissemination
4. **Reach Channels**: Mini program pop-ups, short video platforms, campus promotion

### Effect Metrics
- New customer second-month retention rate ≥ 40%
- First-year consumption frequency ≥ 6 times
- Fission new customer proportion ≥ 20%

---

## Cluster 4: Churn Risk Group
### Customer Characteristics
- Proportion: 14%
- Labels: Extremely low consumption frequency, high historical AOV, older age
- Risk: Former high-value customers, continuously churning

### Operation Objectives
- Churn recall rate ≥ 20%
- Repurchase rate within 3 months after reactivation ≥ 50%

### Specific Strategies
1. **Exclusive Recall Benefits**
   - Issue exclusive large-value return coupons with low threshold and high intensity
   - Phone follow-up: Manual follow-up for high-value churned customers to understand reasons
2. **Emotional Reactivation**
   - Push "Long time no see" themed benefits with historical consumption memory points
   - Exclusive greetings + benefits on store anniversaries and festivals
3. **Return Undertaking**
   - Secondary touch within 30 days after return, issue repurchase coupons
   - Push new products matching their historical preferred categories
4. **Reach Channels**: Recall SMS, manual phone calls, targeted emails

### Effect Metrics
- Churn recall rate ≥ 20%
- 3-month repurchase rate after recall ≥ 50%
- Recall ROI ≥ 1:2.5
